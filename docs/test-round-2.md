# Clicky test round 2 (11 Oct 2026)

Installed fresh on the ASUS, settings and keys kept. Talk key: Right Shift. Write key: Right Alt.

## 1. The companion
- Hold **Right Shift**: a soft start sound plays and the original sound bars appear next to the triangle. Let go: an end sound plays. While it works, the triangle spins with a light running around it.
- Only one triangle should ever be on screen, on either Zenbook screen.
- Ask "where is the save button?": it flies there and points. Some answers also draw an arrow or a box.

## 2. Write mode (Right Alt)
1. Open a Gmail reply. Hold **Right Alt** and say "reply to this, say thanks and ask what they use it for". The text streams in at the cursor, and it's never sent.
2. Hold Right Alt again and say "shorter". It rewrites the reply in place.
3. **Tap** Right Alt once. The text is undone.
4. Select a paragraph, hold Right Alt and say "make this more formal". The selection is replaced.
5. In Notepad, hold Right Alt and dictate "today I need to do three things, first…, second…, third…". It comes out as a numbered list.
6. While you're dictating, the triangle sits beside your text cursor.

## 3. Agent mode ("Clickey, …")
1. Say "Clickey, open the vscode app". The first time, the rules screen opens. Read it and press **I understand, turn on agent mode**.
2. Say it again. A bar at the top shows each step. The triangle lands on each target before clicking. **Esc** or Stop ends the task, and moving the mouse pauses it.
3. "Clicky, where is the export button?" should still get a normal answer, not an agent task.
4. "can you open LinkedIn and scroll my feed" (no name) offers first. Press Right Shift to let it go ahead.

## 4. Today and tasks
- The Today page greets you by name and has a Tasks box. Add "open Notepad and type hello", then leave the mouse alone for about 5 seconds. You get a notification, it runs, and a second notification says it's done.

## 5. Writes like me
- After a few writes in Gmail or WhatsApp, "What Clicky has learned" fills in with notes about how you write. Each note can be removed. There's also a Clear button, a switch to pause learning, and a never-learn app list.

## 6. AI and voice
- Choose a voice: press ▶ on any voice to hear it and use it. The speed slider goes up to 2×.
- Key sounds: Soft, Bubble, Glass or Off, with a ▶ preview.
- Answers use free OpenRouter models, with free backups when one is busy.
