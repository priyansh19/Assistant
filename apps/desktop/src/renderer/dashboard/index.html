Privacy
Clicky only listens and takes a screenshot while you hold the key. Audio goes to your speech-to-text provider, the screenshot and question go to your answer model, and the answer text goes to your voice provider. Screenshots and audio are never saved; history is text only and stays on this PC.

Let Clicky do tasks for you

Say "Clickey, …" and Clicky does it with your mouse and keyboard while you watch, for example "Clickey, open Notepad and write a shopping list". Before it starts, here's how it behaves:

  • It shows every step first: the triangle flies to the spot, and a bar at the top shows what it's doing.
  • It always asks before sending, buying, deleting, posting or submitting anything.
  • It never types passwords, card numbers or codes, and it stops at sign-in screens.
  • Esc or the Stop button stops it at once. If you use the mouse or keyboard, it pauses and asks.
  • It checks that every step worked before the next one, and never touches the apps you list below.
