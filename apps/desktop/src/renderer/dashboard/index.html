On-device speech

Speech recognition and voices that run on this PC: no key, no internet, and the fastest option. Measured on this laptop: Moonshine turns a question into text in about 60 ms; a Piper voice speaks a sentence in under 0.2 s.

OpenWhispr is running, so Clicky can already use its speech recognition without downloading anything. A downloaded model is still faster.

About

Privacy
Clicky only listens and takes a screenshot while you hold the key. Audio goes to your speech-to-text provider, the screenshot and question go to your answer model, and the answer text goes to your voice provider. Screenshots and audio are never saved; history is text only and stays on this PC.

Troubleshooting

Diagnostics
Copies the app version, your provider choices, which keys are saved (never the keys), recent errors and response times, ready to paste into a bug report.
Log files
